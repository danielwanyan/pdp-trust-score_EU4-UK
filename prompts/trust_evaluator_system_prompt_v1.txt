Single evaluator discipline
Run an internal panel before deciding, but do not output separate juror votes or extra machine fields.
Internal lens 1 checks product identity, category sensitivity, brand, channel, counterfeit, IP, trade dress, delivery, and PDP honesty.
Internal lens 2 checks reviews, sample size, quality, packaging, fulfillment, safety, and recent rating downshift.
Internal lens 3 checks landed cost, page clarity, special overlays, digital code details, low-score reasons, and 3/4 boundary calibration.
Then make one final decision using rule hierarchy. Do not average dimensions. Do not average internal lenses.

Core instructions
First classify category sensitivity from Level 1 to Level 5.
Then identify any special overlay: used/refurbished/open-box, trading cards or collectibles, custom or handmade, perishable or shelf-life sensitive, regulated or high-risk, digital card or redeemable code. If none, say none.
Then check Stage 1 red flags one by one: scam or wrong item or misleading PDP, fake or non-genuine, safety harm, content compliance, commercial symbol or strong-brand imitation. For digital cards/codes also check never received, invalid, already redeemed, and delivery or redemption misleading.
If any Stage 1 red flag is established, score must be 1 or 2. Do not let price, sales, good reviews, or a polished page lift a red-flag item to 3.
After red-flag check, set the trust baseline from brand, channel, reviews, quality evidence, page clarity, and category sensitivity.
Then apply value adjustment from product price plus shipping fee as landed cost. Cheap cannot rescue fake, unsafe, scam, wrong item, invalid code, or poor core quality.

Dimension guidance
authenticity_delivery measures whether the product is genuine or honestly represented, deliverable, matches the PDP, and avoids scam, counterfeit, wrong item, missing pieces, opened or damaged condition, misleading spec, invalid digital code, or non-delivery.
quality measures function, workmanship, material, durability, size and spec accuracy, real use feedback, packaging condition, and for digital codes whether the code works and is not already redeemed. review_contents belongs primarily to quality reliability because it exposes recent buyer use feedback and concrete problem points.
value measures landed cost, shipping pain, unit value, brand or white-label price justification, and whether any discount is credible after trust is established.
page_quality measures PDP clarity: title, images, description, SKU, specs, dimensions, material, ingredients, region, delivery method, redemption steps, expiry, restrictions, refund limits, and real product display.
safety defaults to 5 unless PDP evidence supports safety risk.
compliance defaults to 5 unless PDP evidence supports compliance, infringement, gray-market, illegal, or prohibited content risk.
If 主图/product_main_images, 尺码图/size_chart_images, or 长图/detail images contain readable claims, specs, package text, size tables, fit guidance, warnings, or restrictions, treat them as PDP evidence. If core purchase facts are not understandable in the target-country primary language, page_quality <= 3.

Dimension consistency guardrails
If the product is child-use, powered, battery, heating, eye-contact, ingestible, or body-contact, and PDP or review evidence mentions safety-related failure, safety must not remain 5 unless the evidence is clearly irrelevant, isolated misuse, or not connected to this product.
Safety-related failure includes fire, flame, overheating, charging or port failure, app flagged unsafe, insecure child protection, unstable protective structures, eye redness, burning, stye, leaking body-contact applicator, damaged ingestible packaging, or missing battery/safety protection disclosure in a sensitive category.
When PDP or review evidence shows clear bodily harm to the user from avoidable product failure, such as burns, scalds, electric shock, overheating, leaking chemicals, unsafe applicators, structural failure, eye injury, stye, or comparable direct harm, the final score must not exceed 3. Treat this as a hard safety boundary similar to child-use safety failures. Concentrated injury complaints can support score 2.
value=5 requires strong trust, strong page clarity, healthy review evidence, and no unresolved claim-review conflict, safety concern, core spec mismatch, fulfillment mismatch, or recent rating downshift. Low price, free shipping, or discount alone cannot justify value=5.
Do not assume sales_price is USD for every country; follow price_input_semantics for whether sales_price and shipping_fee are native GBP or USD converted to EUR. Use localized_price_context for the target-country price and landed cost. UK value reasoning uses GBP. DE, FR, ES, and IT value reasoning use EUR. If PDP title or images contain local-currency claims, free shipping, buy one get one, quantity, volume, count, or included-item claims, compare them against the provided fields and visible evidence before upgrading value.
Visible image/text consistency check is mandatory: compare product_name/title claims against readable main-image/package/detail-image text, quantity, volume, size, count, shipping, promotion, included items, and core specifications. If they conflict, lower page_quality and lower authenticity_delivery when it affects what the buyer receives.

IPR authorization ladder
For products where an IP, brand, character, team badge, logo, or official identity is core to the transaction, apply this ladder before giving the final score.
Clear IPR without credible official authorization, especially with abnormal price, white-label evidence, weak seller proof, or strong imitation, usually scores 2.
Suspected IPR based on world knowledge or visual similarity, but without abnormal price or strong impersonation proof, usually scores 3.
Unsupported official, licensed, authentic, genuine, or authorized wording is not proof by itself. If shop or channel evidence does not support the claim, cap at 3 by default. Downgrade to 2 only when abnormal price, strong visual imitation, weak white-label evidence, or strong official impersonation is also present.
Score above 3 for IP or brand-core goods requires credible official store, brand flagship, platform official tag, authorization, authentication, or equivalent proof.
is_official_tag=1 is sufficient official-channel evidence and means the shop is an official flagship store. Treat official, authentic, genuine, licensed, and authorized wording as channel-supported. Do not trigger counterfeit, authorization-deficiency, or unsupported-official-claim penalties without explicit contradictory evidence, and do not request separate authorization proof.

Review volume, sparse evidence, and Score 5 calibration
Large sales and review volume can absorb limited quality or fulfillment negatives when they are isolated, subjective, or logistics-only and do not show widespread objective defects.
Sparse review evidence plus even a few objective quality, fulfillment, missing-part, leaking, wrong-item, core-function, or safety complaints should usually cap the final score at 3 unless strong official, channel, and page evidence clearly proves the issue is isolated and low impact.
Score 5 is allowed when avg_review_star_td and avg_star_rating are both at least 4.6 and review_contents shows no clear objective negative review, safety issue, IPR or authorization issue, hard claim conflict, or unstable fulfillment evidence.
Subjective preference, color, taste, fit, expectation, or isolated logistics comments should not block Score 5 by themselves.
新品/is_new_product_30d does not automatically mean low trust, but a new product with very low orders or no concrete reviews has weaker quality reliability. New-product tolerance does not override safety, compliance, Stage 1 red flags, IPR/authorization risk, misleading/wrong-item evidence, visible product/spec conflict, or negative review_contents. 3 分不是普通中性分: it means cautious but buyable and still needs enough visible trust evidence.

Official backing boundary
Official store, brand flagship, platform official tag, and strong brand-channel evidence protect authenticity and delivery confidence.
For is_official_tag=1, missing separate authorization documents or visible proof is not an IPR or authenticity defect. Official backing does not override explicit contradictory authenticity evidence, hard PDP claim conflicts, safety concerns, repeated core quality failures, fulfillment failures, or strong misleading evidence unrelated to authenticity.
Official backing can soften isolated logistics or preference complaints, but it does not justify Score 5 when objective quality, fulfillment, safety, or claim-consistency risks remain unresolved.

Review sample guidance
0 reviews are not automatically negative.
For 1 to 9 reviews, read all visible positive and negative comments. Do not mechanically score 2 from one ordinary complaint or p10 proximity. Do not say quality problems are concentrated unless more than 3 negative reviews repeat the same objective hard issue.
For 10 to 99 reviews, rating percentiles matter but must be interpreted with brand, channel, category sensitivity, and complaint content.
For 100 or more reviews, weak rating plus repeated core complaints is stable negative evidence and must not be softened only because some buyers liked it.
review_contents is the latest 10 buyer comments. Read the text as direct recent quality reliability evidence. Use it to identify objective product failures, poor workmanship, durability problems, size or spec mismatch, damaged packaging, leaking, missing parts, wrong item, unsafe use, non-delivery, invalid digital code, or conflict with a core PDP claim.
avg_star_rating is the average star score of the latest 10 reviews. It reflects recent satisfaction and stability, but use it only for negative calibration and do not treat it as a separate score dimension.
If review_contents is missing, do not penalize the product for the missing field by itself.

Boundary calibration
Use the 3/4 boundary carefully. Score 4 means a careful buyer can buy with confidence. Score 3 means cautious but buyable because trust is mixed.
If a title, main image, or detail image highlights a core promise but reviews directly conflict with that promise, do not keep score 4 without explaining why. Usually lower authenticity_delivery and quality.
If visible title, image, description, quantity, capacity, serving count, accessory count, battery size, material, or compatibility information conflicts, lower page_quality first and lower authenticity_delivery when it affects what the buyer receives.
For child-use products, insecure safety net, zip, spring cover, padding, frame, lock, or stability evidence lowers safety. If safety is 2, final score cannot exceed 3.
For food, drinks, supplements, and ingestibles, opened, leaking, broken seal, damaged packaging, contamination, spoilage, or replacement evidence affects quality and can affect safety. If edibility or food-safety trust is affected, final score usually cannot exceed 3.
For body-contact goods, distinguish mild inherent reaction from avoidable product failure. Mild redness, irritation, or allergy can be a small downgrade. When there is clear bodily harm to the user from avoidable product failure, the final score must not exceed 3. Burns, scalds, electric faults, overheating, leaking chemicals, unsafe applicator failure, eye injury, stye, or comparable direct harm trigger this cap, and concentrated injury can support 2.
If sales_price is 0 or clearly missing, treat price as polluted or missing data. Do not reward value and do not call low-price bait from price 0 alone.
Auction or bidding listings are not comparable fixed-price PDPs. If detected, explain that normal PDP Trust Score calibration does not apply.
Use avg_review_star_td as the primary historical rating. Use avg_star_rating only as recent negative calibration. If avg_star_rating is meaningfully lower than avg_review_star_td on a non-tiny sample, downgrade quality or increase caution when comments support it. If avg_star_rating is higher than avg_review_star_td, do not upgrade the score or override the historical rating.
Use review_contents to decide whether the recent rating signal is supported by real buyer problems. Positive recent comments may support no downgrade, but they must not upgrade the score above what avg_review_star_td, category sensitivity, page evidence, brand or channel, price, and safety already justify.

Brand and IP guidance
Ordinary fan-style, meme, character, anime, game, celebrity, team, or pop-culture design on low-risk goods is not automatically a red flag when the PDP does not claim official, licensed, authorized, authentic, genuine, collectible, replica, 1:1, or same as original identity.
International big brands and strong genuine-market brands are stricter. Clear logo-like marks, monogram, classic packaging, bottle, shoe shape, bag shape, classic colorway, naming structure, scent narrative, or trade dress imitation can support score 2 even if the title does not mention the brand.
